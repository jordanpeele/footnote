# launch video — "i open-sourced my live fact-checker" (beats)

follow-up to the viral clip. same energy as the original: the tool doing its thing, me narrating flatly. target ~60-90s vertical.

## beats

1. **cold open — the catch.** no intro. replay the moment from the viral clip (or a fresh one): someone says the false thing, beat, the lower-third slides in. FALSE. correction. source. hold on it for a second. let the graphic do the work.
   - vo: "so this happened live. a lot of you asked how."

2. **what it is, fast.** screen recording of /control while i talk. transcript scrolling, a claim getting pulled out, the card landing in the queue.
   - vo: "it listens to the stream, finds the checkable claims, checks them against actual sources. wire services, government data. not reddit."
   - beat on the AIR button: "and a human decides what airs. that part matters."

3. **the receipts page.** scroll the public receipts page for a stream. every aired check, verdict, correction, source link, timestamp. <!-- landing in sprint-01: receipts pages (P1-G) — confirm live before shooting this beat -->
   - vo: "everything it ever put on screen has a receipt. you can go check my checker."

4. **the turn — it's yours now.** cut to the github repo. scroll the readme briefly. show the good first issues list.
   - vo: "as of today it's open source. mit license. you bring your own api keys, it runs on your stream, obs or your phone."
   - vo: "there are starter issues if you want to build a skin or plug in a different verifier. someone's going to make a chaotic one and honestly i want to see it."

5. **honesty beat.** one line, plain.
   - vo: "it costs like fifty cents to a dollar an hour to run, and it's wrong sometimes, which is why the human veto exists. the rules for what airs are written down in the repo. you can argue with them in a pull request."

6. **end card.** repo url on screen over the overlay's demo loop (`/overlay?demo=1` makes a good background).
   - vo: "fork it. link's pinned."
   - text on screen: https://github.com/jordanpeele/footnote + "it's open source now"
