## 3 · Commit-author email — the explicit answer

`git log --all --format='%ae | %ce'` → a single identity: **`cobyweiss@icloud.com`**
(author and committer, all history). This is the operator's deliberate choice
(Vercel rejects commits with unroutable author emails; documented preference).
It is his real, personal iCloud address and it is public the instant anyone reads
the repo.

- **Not a blocker.** The operator is a public figure who ships under his own name;
  the email is consistent with that public identity and the account is his.
- **Remediation IF he wants the personal address off future commits:**
  `git config user.email "<numeric-id>+jordanpeele@users.noreply.github.com"`
  (find the id at github.com/settings/emails; enable "Keep my email addresses
  private"). This affects only new commits.
- **Removing it from the 26 existing commits** = history rewrite (git-filter-repo,
  force-push, invalidates every existing clone/SHA). **Escalated, not executed** —
  operator's call, and almost certainly not worth it for his own public email.
