# Street protocol — the operator's rulebook

Print this. It fits on a page on purpose. Gear, arming, and the go/no-go list live in
[STREET_CHECKLIST.md](./STREET_CHECKLIST.md) (maintained separately) — run that before
you leave the house. This doc is the editorial rules once you're rolling.

## The rules

1. **Veto everything (D15).** Auto-air is OFF. Nothing reaches the broadcast without
   your thumb. The machine nominates; you air. Every card, every time.

2. **Don't air Unverifiable (R41).** You keep the discretion, but the norm is DON'T —
   an Unverifiable card tells viewers nothing and spends the chyron's credibility. If
   you air one anyway, know why you did.

3. **Card text ≠ what was said → SKIP (D17, interim).** If the card's claim text is not
   the sentence the speaker actually asserted — polarity flipped, wrong entity,
   anything — skip it. A correct verdict does not save a card that misquotes the
   moment. This rule retires when P7-A ships spoken-framing display.

4. **Off record = mute.** When someone asks to go off record, or the conversation
   shouldn't be checked: mute the feed (the /op mute button, once P7-C lands). Until
   then: stop talking, HOLD everything, let the queue die quietly. Silence in, silence
   out.

## Why (FS-1, 2026-08-10)

The speaker said "**NO** woman has run a mile faster than four minutes" — true.
Every internal stage was correct, and the card still aired as *"A woman has run a mile
faster than four minutes" ✓ TRUE* — a false sentence wearing a TRUE badge.
The pipeline can be right and the card can still lie. Your thumb is the last gate.
